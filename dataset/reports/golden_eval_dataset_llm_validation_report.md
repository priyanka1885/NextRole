## 2. What Was Validated and Fixed

- **Auto-fixed:** claimed `matching_skills` with no literal word-level trace in the resume text were removed and reclassified as `missing_skills` -- a factual correction (the claim had zero evidence), safe to apply mechanically.
- **Flagged, not auto-fixed:** records where the LLM's own decision looks inconsistent with its own listed evidence (e.g. `Good Fit` with 2+ listed missing critical requirements, or `No Fit` with zero identified gaps). Overriding a Good/Potential/No Fit call is a judgment call, not a safe mechanical fix -- these are listed below for a human (or a follow-up LLM re-pass) to review.
- **`expected_decision` preservation:** cross-checked against `annotated_dataset.csv` -- confirmed unchanged (still the original source label) for every record.
- Failed LLM calls were retried against the *same* already-sampled record only; no replacement record was drawn from the rest of the dataset.
